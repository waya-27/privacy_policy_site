Article 2 (Description of Service)

The App provides digital detox features designed to encourage users to voluntarily stop watching short-form videos through gradual screen dimming:

  • Detection of short-form video screens on YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels, and similar apps
  • Progressive overlay filter display based on viewing duration
  • Timer display and action buttons in the notification bar (+3 min extend, Pause, Open Settings)
  • Customizable timer and filter intensity settings
  • Custom monitored app addition (Premium plan)
  • Google AdMob advertising (Settings screen only)
