Article 2 (Service Overview)

Prompt Beacon is an AI prompt logging tool for developers and creators. Key features include:

  • Prompt logging: Record AI prompt details with hashtags, app tags, result notes, and status
  • Status management: Track prompts as In Progress, Done, or Failed

All data is stored locally on your device. The App runs completely offline and requires no internet connection.
